Station map

Every station the page reports on. Arrows fly with the wind, coloured by where it comes from: upslope (SW–NW), trades, or drainage at night (NE–SE), other. The red dashed line marks the shear line, drawn through the middle of the stations that bracket it. Tap a station for its latest reading.

Where the numbers come from. Station readings: UH Hawaiʻi Mesonet, MECO and RAWS stations on the slope, via api.weather.gov, the Hawaiʻi Climate Data Portal and WRCC. Sunlight is measured and compared with a clear-sky estimate. The shear line comes from the wind at stations from Paia up to Keokea. Forecasts: GFS sunlight, Open-Meteo surface air and Kahului forecast soundings (BUFKIT).

A hobby project. Updated about every 15 minutes from 06:00 to 18:45 HST, and hourly overnight for the forecast. Flight logs (IGC files) from Kula would make it better.
